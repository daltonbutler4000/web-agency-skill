# Surface brief: Solell home page (`site/src/pages/index.astro`)

Scope: the whole one-page site. Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: a buyer of a second home in Ibiza or Mallorca, comparing agencies, often on a
phone. Action: send a private enquiry. Proof: six named homes described with judgement; a clear
four-step process; a named contact. Constraints: nothing invented presented as fact; stand-in
photographs credited; form sends nothing.

Memorable moment: each home's italic name sitting on a paper tab cut into its photograph.

Unresolved: final photographs for the six homes (stand-ins now; generation prompts in
`agency/06-content.md`).

## Direction contract

THESIS: An estate agency that reads like a small almanac: six homes, each given a name and a
sentence before any figure. It refuses the category opening of an aerial photograph with a
search box and a portfolio count.

OWN-WORLD: Chalk-limewash ground, sand second ground, one terracotta that owns a whole section
and every action, warm brown-black ink. Newsreader light with italics for voice; Hanken Grotesk
for facts. Photographs framed 4:5 with captions beneath; italic titles on paper tabs overlapping
the frame; hairline rules; square corners.

STORY: The visitor understands Solell keeps a short list on purpose, believes the agency knows
its houses and the islands, and enquires privately about one home or with a general brief.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Split. Left on chalk: headline "Houses on the sunlit side of two islands." at
about 88px with "sunlit side" in italic, a two-sentence statement, "Enquire privately" and "See
the six homes". Right on sand: one 4:5 framed photograph of a limewashed wall with a vertical
italic caption beside it and a split caption beneath. Header above with wordmark, descriptor,
anchors and the enquiry action.

FORM: Editorial one-pager. seed: none (pinned) "Go with A, generate the photos, switch to
impeccable" (designer approval of direction A · Almanac, agency/04-art-direction.md).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
