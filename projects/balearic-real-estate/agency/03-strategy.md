# Strategy: one page, one action

Status: **for review** · 2026-11-23 · Inputs: `01-brief.md`, `02-research/findings.md`

## Sitemap

A single page, `/`, with anchored sections. No other pages. The privacy notice and image
credits open in place (a disclosure in the footer), so nothing leaves the page.

## Page objective

**Understand:** this is a small agency that knows a few exceptional homes personally, on two
islands, and deals discreetly. **Do:** send a private enquiry.

**Primary action:** "Enquire privately". It appears in the header at all times, under every
home, and as the closing section. On phones it stays reachable without scrolling back up.

**Positioning to write from:** judgement, not inventory. The research shows competitors sell
volume and search; with six homes we sell selection. No search box, no portfolio counts.

## Sections, in order

| # | Section | Its job | Content needed (none exists yet) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Header** | Name the agency, signpost both islands, keep the enquiry one tap away | Wordmark; 4 anchors (Homes, Islands, Approach, Enquire) |
| 2 | **Opening** | Say in one sentence what the agency is and for whom; set the tone before anything is read | Masthead, one-sentence statement, one framed photograph with a caption |
| 3 | **Point of view** | Give the reason to choose this agency over a network: a short list, kept on purpose | 60–80 words; no statistics |
| 4 | **Selected homes** | Proof of taste. Each home has a name, a place and a sentence before any figures | 6 homes (3 Ibiza, 3 Mallorca): title, village or bay, one sentence, 3 facts (bedrooms, built area, plot), guide price, 1–2 photographs, "Enquire about this home" |
| 5 | **The two islands** | Show local knowledge and help a buyer who has not chosen an island yet | Ibiza and Mallorca side by side: 50 words each, 3–4 named areas each, one photograph each |
| 6 | **How we work** | Remove the unknowns of buying abroad; make the first step feel small | 4 steps: a conversation, a shortlist (including homes not publicly listed), viewings arranged around the buyer, support through purchase |
| 7 | **Enquiry** | Convert. Short enough to finish on a phone | Form: name, email, phone (optional), island (Ibiza / Mallorca / either), message. Direct email and phone beside it. Success and error states. One line on privacy |
| 8 | **Footer** | Honesty and housekeeping | Concept disclaimer, image credits, privacy disclosure, languages note |

Order rationale: homes come before the islands and the process because a buyer comparing
agencies wants evidence first. The point of view sits above the homes so the small number
reads as a choice, not a shortage.

## The journey that matters

Arrives from a recommendation or a search, on a phone → reads the opening and sees one
photograph → scrolls the homes → taps "Enquire about this home" → the form opens with that
home already named in the message → sends → sees a clear confirmation.

Secondary: no home fits → reads "How we work" → enquires with a general brief.

## Decisions taken here (say if you disagree)

1. **Six homes, three per island.** Enough to show range, few enough to show selection.
2. **Guide prices are shown**, marked illustrative, with one home "price on request". Buyers
   expect a figure; hiding all prices reads as evasive.
3. **No testimonials, awards, press logos or sales figures.** They would have to be invented.
   Trust comes from specific writing, named places and a clear process.
4. **A named contact, no portrait.** A fictional person with a name and a direct line makes
   the enquiry feel personal without a stock headshot.
5. **Home-specific enquiry links prefill the form** instead of opening separate pages.

## Imagery plan

About 12 images: 1 opening, 6–9 homes, 2 islands, 1 process or detail. Subjects follow the
research: materials, light, white walls and shadow, people at ease; no aerial bays or
infinity pools as openers. Unsplash first, generated where nothing suitable exists; each
logged with source and licence.

## Content model

None. Homes are a small structured list in the code so they stay consistent and could move
to a CMS later.

## Not in this page

Search, filters, property detail pages, map, blog, newsletter, seller valuation, live chat.
