## Failed and fixed during QA

- Phone action bar outside a landmark (accessibility scan): fixed.
- Seven problems and six inconsistencies from the critique: fixed, see `07-critique.md`.

## Open

- **Photographs are stand-ins.** Final images are needed before this could be shown as finished.
- **Open Graph image uses a relative address.** It needs the real domain once there is one.
- **No sitemap or canonical address**, because there is no domain and the page is `noindex`.
- **Seven preferences** from the critique await a decision.

## Not tested

- **Safari and Firefox.** Only Chromium ran here. The scrolling title animation uses a newer
  CSS feature; a fallback script covers browsers without it, but that path was not exercised
  in a real Safari or Firefox.
- **Real devices and assistive technology.** No physical phone, no screen reader session.
- **Interaction responsiveness (INP).** Not measurable without real user input; blocking time
  is reported instead.
- **A real form submission.** The form sends nothing by design.
- **The opening animation review by the design lead's motion reviewer.** Its capture tool
  cannot record a page-load entrance; I checked the animation frame by frame myself instead.
- **CMS.** There is none.

## Recommendation

**Go**, as a concept build with stand-in photography. **No-go** as a finished portfolio piece
until the final photographs are in and Safari has been checked once by hand.
