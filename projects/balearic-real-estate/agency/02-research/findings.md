# Research findings, round 1

23 November 2026 · Board: `board.html` · Evidence record: `references.json`

## Category: what competitors do

Sources are search results and the home pages captured for the board.

- **Networks dominate the search results.** Engel & Völkers, Knight Frank, Sotheby's and
  BARNES appear for both islands. Engel & Völkers Ibiza opens with a search box, a pool
  photograph and "Find your dream property in Ibiza" (captured, X1).
- **Local agencies copy the portal pattern.** Balearic Properties leads with a drone shot, a
  search field and "more than 2,000" properties (captured, X2). Baerz & Co uses an aerial
  photograph, a serif headline and a search field (captured, X3).
- **Volume is the usual claim.** Size of portfolio, number of offices, years in business.
- **"Selective" and "off-market" are the boutique claims.** Ibiza Agents is described as
  selective and relationship-led with an in-house concierge; Spain Sotheby's says it specialises
  in off-market properties (search results only, sites not opened).
- **One competitor already looks like a design studio:** Domus Nova (London and Ibiza), which
  frames interiors on white and writes a sentence about each home's character (captured, A1–A3).
  A search result also lists a BARNES Ibiza partnership with Domus Nova.

**Weakness to exploit:** almost everyone sells inventory. Nobody on the board except Domus Nova
sells judgement. A one-pager with six homes cannot compete on inventory and should not try.

## Conventions to keep, clichés to drop

Keep: price or "price on request" visible with each home; location named to the village or
bay; a human contact, not only a form; both islands clearly signposted.

Drop: hero search box; aerial bay and infinity-pool openers; portfolio-size claims;
"dream property"; partner portraits in suits; gold, black and script lettering.

## What the references teach

| Direction | References | Idea |
|---|---|---|
| Gallery White | Domus Nova, Moredesign, Blakstad | White ground, small type, photographs framed as objects |
| Almanac | Inigo, White Ibiza | The agency as a publication: serif italics or a masthead, tinted grounds, homes with titles |
| One Colour | Hotel Corazón | One saturated ground, one typeface in capitals |
| Full-Bleed Cinema | Camper & Nicholsons, Le Collectionist | Image fills the screen, interface nearly invisible |

Principles that recur across the stronger references:

1. Character comes from one decisive choice (a typeface, a colour, a way of framing), not many.
2. Photographs of materials, light and people outperform views and pools.
3. Each home gets a name and a sentence before any specification.
4. Text sits on a clean ground, not over a busy photograph.
5. Small type against generous space reads as confidence; it must still pass contrast.

**Agency recommendation (proposal):** Almanac as the base, the framing of Gallery White, and a
single warm colour in the spirit of One Colour. Leave out Full-Bleed Cinema: it depends on
footage a concept project does not have.

## Designer feedback

2026-11-23, verbatim: "Go with your recommendation, move on to strategy"

No individual likes or dislikes were given. The recommendation above is therefore the chosen
direction to carry into art direction: Almanac base (Inigo, White Ibiza), Gallery White framing
(Domus Nova, with Moredesign's materials imagery and Blakstad's white-wall subject), one warm
colour in the spirit of Hotel Corazón. Full-Bleed Cinema is out.
