# Discovery: interview and brief

Goal: a one-to-two page brief the designer approves, good enough to direct research.

## Before asking anything

Read what already exists: the message, attached documents, the repository, `PRODUCT.md`,
the current site if there is one. Ask only for what is missing. Ask in batches of at most five
questions, most consequential first, and offer a suggested answer where you can infer one. Two
rounds is normally enough; state your assumptions rather than running a third.

## What the brief must answer

1. **The business:** what is offered, to whom, and what makes it different.
2. **The job of the site:** the single most important outcome (enquiry, booking, sale, sign-up,
   credibility) and how success will be recognised.
3. **Audience:** who visits, in what situation, on what device, knowing what already.
4. **Scope:** pages or sections, languages, features (forms, booking, shop, blog, search).
5. **Content:** what exists (copy, photography, video, logo, brand guidelines) and what must be
   created. Missing imagery is the most common cause of a weak result; settle it now.
6. **Brand and tone:** existing identity and its flexibility; three adjectives for how it should
   feel; sites the client likes or dislikes and why.
7. **Constraints:** stack or CMS already in use, integrations, hosting, accessibility or legal
   requirements, budget for licensed assets, deadline.
8. **Mode and involvement:** A or B, and which decisions the designer wants to see.

For a redesign, add: what works today and must stay, what is failing, and any data that shows it.

## Push back here

If the goal is vague ("look more modern"), the audience is "everyone", or the scope does not fit
the deadline, say so now and propose a sharper version. A weak brief costs more later.

## Write `agency/01-brief.md`

Sections: Summary (three sentences) · Goal and success measure · Audience · Scope · Content and
assets · Brand and tone · Constraints · Mode · Assumptions (explicitly marked) · Open questions.

## Checkpoint

Present the brief and wait for approval. Approval commits to: the goal, the audience, the scope
and the mode. Log it in `agency/decisions.md`. If Impeccable will be used, run `impeccable init`
afterwards so it records the same facts in `PRODUCT.md` without a second interview.
