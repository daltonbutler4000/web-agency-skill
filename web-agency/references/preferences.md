# The designer's working preferences

Global preferences only. No client, employer or project details belong in this file; those live
in each project's `agency/` folder. A project's brief and brand always outrank this file.

**Status:** every entry is *stated by the designer when the skill was set up (2026-11-23) and not
yet confirmed in practice*. The first time one materially affects a decision, check it in one
sentence ("You've said you prefer X; applying that here"). When the designer confirms, corrects
or adds a preference, offer to update this file, and move confirmed entries under **Confirmed**
with the date. Never record a preference inferred from a single project.

## Stated, unconfirmed

**Design taste**
- Modern, distinctive, polished work over generic templates.
- Cares most about typography, spacing, visual hierarchy, consistent components and thoughtful
  animation.
- Researches exceptional websites before committing to an aesthetic.

**Tools and stack**
- Uses Figma, with growing interest in designing directly in code.
- Works with Astro, Sanity and Claude Code, and with reusable design systems.
- Astro is the preferred starting point for content-focused sites; choose per project.

**Build quality**
- Responsive, performant, accessible.
- Reusable components, design tokens, clean maintainable code.

**Collaboration**
- Wants weak design decisions challenged, not agreed with.
- Approves creative directions and important decisions; does not want to manage minor
  implementation details.
- Prefers concise, practical answers with clear next steps.

## Observed in the designer's bookmarks, unconfirmed

The agent's reading of about 130 bookmarked sites ([bookmarks.md](bookmarks.md), 2026-11-24).
It is an inference from what was saved, not something the designer said. Check before relying
on any line, and delete the ones the designer rejects.

- Full-bleed photography with a light display serif and very little interface, mostly on
  Mediterranean hotels and retreats. This is the largest single group.
- Serif display headlines on software and finance sites, where a grotesk is the usual choice.
- Warm neutral and cream grounds more often than pure white.
- Dark, restrained developer and AI sites built around one product-interface panel.
- Loud colour-block pages with very large type appear, but far less often.

- How much motion is "thoughtful" versus too much.
