Let's find you the
perfect office

OfficeFreedom is the worlds largest marketplace for offices.
Over 15,000 clients have used us to find offices for free.

What sort of office are you looking for?

Big or small, city centre or out of town, funky or traditional, we’ve got all bases covered. And if you need help defining your requirements, no problem, it’s what we do.

We’ll do all the leg work so you don’t have to

We’ll scour the market looking for the best offices matching your requirements. Then we’ll shortlist the cream of the crop for you to review.

Let’s go and
check a few out

Once you’ve selected some offices to view, we’ll make all the arrangements. We can accompany you on viewings too if you wish and virtual online tours are easily arranged if you prefer.

We’ll get you the
best deal

When you’ve selected the office you really want, we can negotiate the best price and terms on your behalf. We’ll use our experience, market knowledge and industry contacts to get you the best deal.

And the best bit,
our service is free

We’ve saved the best news until last - our services are completely free of charge. We get our fee from the landlord, once a client is happily settled into their new office.
